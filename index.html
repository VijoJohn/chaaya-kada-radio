Chaaya Kada Radio

An original Malayalam shared-radio concept, visually inspired by the focused, single-station experience of the supplied Saloon reference and reimagined around a rainy Kerala chaayakada.

The 80s, 90s and 2000s are mapped using movie release years. The station clock can place listeners at approximately the same point after they choose to join; YouTube ads, availability and regional rules can make playback differ.

Media is delivered by the visible standard YouTube embedded player. This prototype does not download, extract, proxy or restream YouTube media.

Playback and data handling are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
