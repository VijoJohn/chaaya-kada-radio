Chaaya Kada Radio

An original Malayalam CD-radio experience inspired by Kerala tea shops, monsoon evenings and the focused single-station feeling of Deluxe Saloon.

The decades refer to each song's original film or album release, not the date of its Audius upload. This is a small selection of nostalgic Malayalam melodies; some recordings are later renditions or covers, labelled where known. Upload metadata does not authenticate an original recording.

Music is requested from the official Audius API and played directly through the browser’s standard audio player. There is no video player, scraping, downloading or stream extraction.

The rotating disc uses the artwork supplied with the current track. Artists remain identified and every record links back to its Audius page. API-accessible music is governed by the Audius Open Music License.
